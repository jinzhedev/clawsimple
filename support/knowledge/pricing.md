# ClawSimple Pricing

Reviewed: 2026-09-28. Source: https://clawsimple.com/en#pricing
Repository source: `src/data/pricing.ts`.

## Subscription prices

All amounts below are USD.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Included monthly AI credits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | $29.90 per month | $299.00 per year | $13.50 |
| Max | $44.90 per month | $449.00 per year | $26.00 |

Annual prices are yearly charges, not monthly charges. The current checkout controls the amount payable. Do not infer tax treatment, promotions, refund eligibility, rollover, or proration rules from this table.

## Usage and additional agents

Billing includes the subscription and its included AI/search usage, plus any additional managed usage. New deployments use managed AI and search by default. Extra agents on the same deployment do not create a separate server subscription. They share the deployment's managed usage allowance and can consume it faster. Creating a separate deployment is not the same as adding an agent to an existing deployment.

This knowledge source does not specify a universal per-message price. Model selection and usage affect costs. For an account-specific charge or billing dispute, contact support@clawsimple.com; support chat cannot inspect invoices or authorize refunds.
