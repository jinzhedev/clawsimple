Thanks to [ClawSimple's managed deployment](/) (which configures three optional models), OpenClaw allows you to switch between different AI models on the fly directly in your Telegram bot.

![Switching Models](/images/blog/switching-models.png)

### How to Switch

1.  **Open your bot** in Telegram.
2.  Type the command `/models` and hit send.
3.  You will see a list of available providers:
    *   **Models (clawsimple)**: These are the managed models included in your standard/max plan.
4.  Select a **Provider** to view its models.
5.  Click on any **Model Name** (e.g., `anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.5`) to switch to it immediately.

Your bot will confirm the switch, and all subsequent messages will be processed by the new model.

> **Update (March 2026)**: The `/models` command now supports **BYOM (Bring Your Own Model)**! 
>
> You can now switch models on the fly even if you use your own API key. In the past, you needed to redeploy your bot with new model details, but now it's all handled dynamically.

### BYOM Tips: Multi-Model Routing & Visual Tools

If you bring your own API key, using an OpenAI-compatible gateway such as [KeelRouter](https://keelrouter.com/) lets you switch across chat, coding, and image models from a single balance instead of juggling separate provider accounts (see the [GPT Image 2.6 API pricing guide](https://keelrouter.com/guides/gpt-image-2-6-api-pricing) if your bot also generates images).

For workflows where a general chat model isn't enough—such as decomposing character illustrations or updating copy inside UI captures—pair your bot with specialized MCP or HTTP endpoints like [Unmerge](https://unmerge.app/character-to-layers) for splitting character art into layers and [MakeItSay](https://makeitsay.com/edit-text-in-screenshot) for editing text directly inside screenshots.
